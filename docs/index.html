The concept

Not another model. A better way to use the ones you have.

The first interface is opencode through Herdr. Switchboard is the informative control plane around it: discovering resources, making routes eligible, recording outcomes, and showing why the next move is worth taking.

01 · local first

Use nearby intelligence before paid capacity

Ollama or vLLM serves local models. The system checks fit, freshness, context, and health before a route becomes available.

02 · Jev loop

Automate the hard judgment

Jev classifies intent, context, quality, gaps, route value, and user feedback so the operator does not manually compare every attempt.

03 · useful work

Measure accepted outcomes

Tokens are telemetry. The real metric is what one accepted result costs after retries, rework, review, exceptions, and time.

How it works

A bounded learning loop with a hard safety boundary.

Jev does the routine classification and learning. The controller enforces what no probabilistic result may override: eligibility, reservations, budget, egress, acceptance, and human gates.

The route envelope

Local execution is a real route, not a guess

A route is eligible only when the runtime, model, context, hardware, permissions, egress, and budget all fit the current resource snapshot.

The human boundary

Small questions at consequential moments

Jev writes the concise question. James confirms installation, external spend, uncertain recovery, no-progress continuation, or high-risk action. No answer means pause.

A first useful journey

Start with a game. Prove the economics.

The first real task is a small browser Pong-style game: enough moving parts to expose routing quality, but concrete enough to evaluate without pretending subjective quality is solved.

What success means

Cost per accepted outcome

Not “the cheapest token.” Not “the most activity.” The result must satisfy the acceptance contract, and the full cost of getting there must be visible.

Retries and rework stay under one Goal.
Blocked waiting is reported separately.
A failed Frontier progress claim stops further burn.
Context savings count only if quality and evidence survive.

Pre-release roadmap

Build confidence in layers.

01 · Onboard

Herdr, opencode, endpoints, model store, resource-aware bootstrap.

02 · Prove

Offline fixtures for eligibility, reservations, recovery, evaluation, and bounded actions.

03 · Learn

Jev context selection, feedback classification, cost-per-outcome, and Pong.

04 · Qualify

ComfyUI, Blender, H3, real-time control, corpus MapReduce, and contributor adapters.

Not yet promised

Hosted multi-tenancy and unattended paid automation

This release is local-first and public-safe. Credentials, fleet assumptions, automatic model downloads, autonomous Frontier loops, fine-tuning, and hosted tenancy remain gated until the local safety and outcome evidence are real.
